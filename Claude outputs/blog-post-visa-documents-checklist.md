Documents Required for a Germany Student/Work Visa from Pakistan: Complete Checklist

**Content (copy-paste as plain text):**

```
One important caveat before the checklist

Visa requirements are set by the German Federal Foreign Office and implemented through the German Missions in Pakistan (Islamabad and Karachi) and VFS Global, and small details — required bank balance, processing times, appointment availability — do change from year to year. This checklist covers the documents that have been consistently required for years and gives you a realistic starting point, but always cross-check the current, exact list on the official German Missions Pakistan website or your VFS Global appointment page before you submit, since a missing or outdated document is the single most common reason for delays.

The core documents almost every applicant needs

A valid passport with at least two blank pages and validity covering your intended stay, plus copies of all previous passports if you've had one before.

Completed and signed visa application form (available on the German Missions website), along with the visa application checklist form specific to your visa category.

Recent biometric passport-sized photographs meeting the official specifications (usually two).

Proof of German language proficiency at the level required for your specific program or job — this is where your Goethe or telc certificate comes in, and it needs to match what your university, Ausbildung provider, or employer specifically asked for.

Letter of admission or acceptance from a German university, Ausbildung provider, or a signed employment contract, depending on your visa category.

Proof of financial means for your stay, most commonly through a blocked account (Sperrkonto) showing the required minimum balance — this exact amount is reviewed and adjusted periodically by German authorities, so confirm the current figure on the official Bundesverwaltungsamt or German Missions website rather than relying on last year's number.

Proof of health insurance valid in Germany from your arrival date.

Curriculum Vitae (CV) in the Europass format, along with a motivation letter explaining your study or work plan.

Additional documents specific to students

If you're applying for university admission, you'll also typically need your APS Certificate (Akademische Prüfstelle) if applying from Pakistan for most public universities, since APS verifies the authenticity of your academic documents before the visa process — this step has its own separate timeline, so start it early, ideally months before your intended visa application.

Attested academic transcripts and degree certificates (matriculation through your most recent qualification), often required with HEC attestation for Pakistani applicants.

Additional documents specific to work/Ausbildung applicants

Recognition of your foreign qualification (if your profession requires state recognition, such as healthcare roles), your signed employment or training contract, and sometimes a declaration from your employer confirming the role and salary.

What actually causes delays (from what we see with our own students)

Two things repeatedly trip people up: booking the visa appointment before the language certificate or APS certificate is actually ready, and using an outdated blocked-account amount because it was cross-checked from an old blog post or forum thread instead of the current official page. Start your document collection at least 3-4 months before your intended travel date, and treat the official German Missions Pakistan checklist as the final word over anything else you read, including this post.

If you're currently preparing for your Goethe or telc exam as part of this process, that's exactly the stage we can help most with — a language certificate that matches your visa category's exact requirement is one less thing to worry about when appointment day comes.
```
